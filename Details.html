Our story

From first hello
to forever.

Three moments that brought us here.

2019 · The first hello

A very ordinary Tuesday.

We met at the computer lab in KLD. One was staring blankly at a blank screen trying to code a basic HTML page that wouldn't cooperate; the other walked past, pointed out a missing closing tag, and smirked. We ended up skipping the next class just to grab tapsilog and code together.

2021 · Our favorite teammate

Making a life, day by day.

Life around Dasma became a blur of campus hangouts, surviving hell week projects, and figuring out how to adult. We went from fixing broken layouts in school to cheering each other on through late-night coding grinds, realizing we made a pretty great team.

2025 · The question

And then, forever.

On a quiet evening sitting on the bleachers near the campus grounds where it all started, a phone screen lit up with a hand-coded webpage flashing a simple question: Will you marry me? The answer loaded instantly with a huge yes, ready for a lifetime together.
